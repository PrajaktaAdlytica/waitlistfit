- Six-object model and nine consistent queue cases; the three candidates inside case 001 are not additional queue cases. Report state counts total nine and change with local review.

- Primary endpoint, review-minute / constraint-violation guardrails, feasible/infeasible/unknown denominators, uncertainty, 8 interviews / 2 replay commitments / 1 paid letter + named owner and signed data/security and oversight gates.
- Stop if required resources are absent, or unavailable write-back plus added queue work defeats value. Read access is never treated as booking permission.
